Privacy First Architecture

FacePay Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Overview

FacePay is a secure financial prototype application designed and developed by Aditya Veena Maharaj and Gaurav Mudgal under the academic supervision of Dr. Gaurav Raj at Sharda University (PBL-3 Capstone Course CSP-391). Our core mission is providing uncompromising biometric security for digital UPI transactions while upholding the highest standards of personal privacy.

2. Camera Access & Zero Cloud Biometrics

FacePay requests access to your device's camera strictly for on-device facial liveness verification during transactions exceeding ₹500:

  • 100% On-Device Processing: All facial analysis, eye-blink detection, and Euler-angle head-turn tracking are executed locally on your device via Google ML Kit Face Detection.
  • Zero Transmission: No video recordings, photographic frames, facial coordinate points, or biometric hashes are ever transmitted to any external server, cloud infrastructure, or third party.
  • Immediate Memory Purge: Camera frames are processed strictly in volatile device RAM and are purged immediately when the verification window concludes.

3. Transaction & Wallet Data

FacePay utilizes demonstration financial data for user testing and academic evaluation:

  • Local Storage: Transactions and demo wallet balances are cached on-device using Android SharedPreferences.
  • Cloud Synchronization: When connected, transaction records (timestamp, merchant identifier, amount, and liveness verification status) sync to Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase) via encrypted SSL/TLS channels for cloud backup and review.
  • No Banking Credentials: FacePay does not solicit or store bank account numbers, debit card PINs, CVVs, or real UPI banking credentials.

4. Third-Party Libraries & Security

The application employs standard, industry-vetted SDKs:

  • Google ML Kit: On-device computer vision runtime provided by Google LLC.
  • Google Firebase Core & Cloud Firestore: Encrypted cloud database and crash reporting infrastructure provided by Google LLC.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

Users have complete sovereignty over their data. You can delete all local transaction history and reset your demonstration wallet at any time through the in-app Settings → Clear History / Reset Demo Data button, or by uninstalling the application.
